About This Page

Purpose

Nairobi Wildlife Services (NWS) Welcome to Nairobi Wildlife Services (NWS), where we are committed to the conservation, protection, and sustainable management of Kenya's rich wildlife heritage. We work to safeguard wildlife and their natural habitats while promoting environmental conservation, research, and responsible tourism. Through community engagement, education, and dedicated conservation efforts, NWS strives to ensure that future generations can continue to enjoy Kenya's unique biodiversity. Whether you are visiting our parks, learning about wildlife, or supporting conservation initiatives, we invite you to join us in protecting nature for a better and more sustainable future.
